# R1.1 · Learner Tools (phase spec)

_Status: scoped · Free (on device) · Depends on: R1_

## Goal
Make Learn mode a real study tool: look up words in context and turn them into flashcards.

## Scope
1. **Tap a word** in the Script editor or Learn-mode subtitles → dictionary entry (reading, meanings, part of speech), from a bundled offline Japanese–English dictionary. Candidate: JMdict (CC BY-SA; commercial use allowed with attribution — Lead confirms licence terms and attribution placement).
2. **Save word** with its Line as the context sentence and the Panel crop as the picture.
3. **Export to Anki** (CSV and `.apkg`), and a simple in-app word list.
4. Word segmentation on device (NaturalLanguage tokenizer), with furigana from R1.

## Acceptance criteria
- Lookup works offline in under 200 ms; segmentation correct for ≥90% of golden-set Lines.
- Exported `.apkg` imports into Anki desktop and AnkiMobile with picture, sentence, reading and meaning fields.
- Dictionary attribution shown in Settings → Licences.
