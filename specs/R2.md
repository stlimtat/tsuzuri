# R2 · EPUB Reader + Read-aloud (phase spec)

_Status: scoped · Free (on device) · Depends on: R1.5 (EPUBCore), R1 Read-aloud_

## Goal
A comfortable reader for the user's own EPUBs, with PocketBook-style read-aloud.

## Scope
1. **Rendering:** evaluate the Readium Swift Toolkit (BSD-3 licence; Lead confirms) versus a custom WKWebView renderer in a spike; reflowable and fixed-layout EPUB; Japanese vertical writing and right-to-left page progression.
2. **Reading settings:** fonts, size, margins, line spacing, themes (light, sepia, dark), paged vs scrolling.
3. **Read-aloud:** reuse `ReadAloud`; sentence highlighting synced to speech; auto page turn; sleep timer; background audio with lock-screen controls; speed control.
4. **Learn mode for books:** furigana display option for Japanese text; tap-to-lookup from R1.1.
5. **Navigation:** table of contents, bookmarks, highlights and notes, search in book.
6. **Sync:** reading position, bookmarks and highlights via iCloud.
7. **Library:** books and comics together (one Library), with covers from metadata.

## Acceptance criteria
- Fixture EPUBs (reflowable, fixed layout, vertical Japanese) render correctly on iPhone, iPad and Mac.
- Read-aloud highlights the sentence being spoken with <150 ms drift and continues across chapters and in the background.
- Position, bookmarks and highlights sync between two devices.
- No network use for reading or Read-aloud.
- External pen test before release.

## Open questions
- Cloud neural voices for books: out of scope (cost); revisit only as a paid feature under ADR 0004.
