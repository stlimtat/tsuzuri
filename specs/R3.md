# R3 · Full Metadata Editor (phase spec)

_Status: scoped · Free (on device) · Depends on: R1.5 (EPUBCore)_

## Goal
Calibre-level metadata editing for EPUBs, reimplemented from scratch.

## Scope
1. **All common OPF metadata:** title and sort title, authors and contributors with roles and file-as, series and index, tags/subjects, publisher, publication date, language(s), identifiers (ISBN, UUID, ASIN, others), description (rich text), rating (stored in our model and as a `calibre:rating` meta for compatibility), rights.
2. **EPUB 2 and 3 correctness:** `opf:` attributes vs `<meta refines>`; `dcterms:modified` updated on save; unique identifier preserved.
3. **Covers:** replace, crop, generate a simple cover from title/author.
4. **Web metadata lookup:** search by ISBN or title/author against Google Books and Open Library; side-by-side comparison; pick fields to apply; cover download. No user content is sent beyond the search terms. Lead decides whether calls go direct from the device (keyless APIs) or via our backend (APIs needing a key); a key must never ship in the app.
5. **Bulk edit:** select many books; set or append fields (series with auto-numbering, tags, publisher, language); search and replace in fields with regex; preview before applying; undo.
6. **Save templates:** file naming from metadata (e.g. "{author_sort} - {series} {series_index} - {title}") on export.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every field round-trips for EPUB 2 and 3 fixtures and appears correctly in Apple Books and at least one other reader.
- Bulk edit of 200 books completes in under 10 s on an iPad with full undo.
- Lookup returns results for ≥90% of a 50-ISBN fixture list; nothing beyond search terms leaves the device.
- External pen test before release.
